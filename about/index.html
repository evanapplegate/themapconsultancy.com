What we do

The Map Consultancy quickly delivers data-driven maps at all scales.

If you have real map volume: let us stand up your own tiny in-house cartography shop (usually just one of your designers who loves maps.)

Principal

I’m Evan Applegate, you’ll find my maps in National Geographic, Manhattan offices, at the fête.

I first mapped for a weekly news magazine where I noticed cartography’s advantage over other graphics: instant legibility. You never have to explain to a reader “you’re looking at a map, and it’s trying to tell you something.” Try that with a scatter plot.

People are also happy to see maps. Many tell me they love maps. Anyone you know profess love for a sankey diagram?

So readers are primed to understand and enjoy maps. This is a real inheritance: past cartographers laid a foundation of trust and aesthetics that allows today’s mapmakers to tell stories with maps. When you don’t have to sell the medium, you can get right to the message.
